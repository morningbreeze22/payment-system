What is this & how to use ▾

This is the complete territory a payment can travel — all twelve states, every legal move from the spec's flow table. Pick a story and watch the coin make the journey, told in plain words. Flip on Plain + Tech for the exact state tuple, money moves, and § references at every step. Click any arrow — story or not — to interrogate its rule. One scoping fact before you play: this machine runs per payment, and a trade can carry several payments — a three-payment trade runs three of these maps in parallel, coupled only by the trade's snapshot messages (the stories “Two payments, one trade” and “A trade-mate poisons the snapshot” show exactly that coupling).

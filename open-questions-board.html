Forty-three questions stand between this design and go-live

Every open question about the payment system, grouped by who has to answer it. Each card is self-contained — click it for what's being asked, why it matters, and what breaks if it stays unanswered; no prior reading required. The small references at the bottom of each card point into the requirements document (requirment-v4.md) for anyone who wants the full detail. Each question offers its realistic answer choices — pick one (or choose “Other” and write your own), add context in the comment box, and Submit. Answers are saved in your browser. When the round is done, hit Export answers for a markdown brief of every question and decision — ready to hand straight back to the agent or paste into a ticket. Didn't finish? Import answers loads a previous export (paste or file) and merges it, so a question round can span as many meetings — and browsers — as it needs.
